# China First-Timer Practical Checklist

A short, printable checklist for international visitors planning a first trip to China. Companion to **[Relax in China](https://relaxinchina.com)**.

👉 **Guides: [relaxinchina.com](https://relaxinchina.com)** · Contact: Market@relaxinchina.com

Rules for visas, payments and transport change. Prefer the dated articles on the site and the official issuer pages over this checklist when they disagree.

## What Relax in China is

Relax in China is an English travel site for international tourists. It covers entry and visa rules, how to pay (cards, Alipay, WeChat Pay), getting around (high-speed rail, flights, subway, taxis), what not to do, city guides and classic routes such as the Beijing–Xi'an–Shanghai Golden Triangle, with a focus on less crowded, more relaxing stops.

## Before you fly

- [ ] Confirm whether your passport qualifies for the current visa-free / transit policy, or book a visa appointment.
- [ ] Tell your bank you will travel; carry at least one backup card and a small amount of cash.
- [ ] Install and verify **Alipay** and/or **WeChat Pay** foreign-user flows while you still have home-country SMS.
- [ ] Decide eSIM vs local SIM; download offline maps for your first city.
- [ ] Book long-haul high-speed rail legs early (passport details matter on 12306 / Trip.com-style bookings).
- [ ] Pre-book timed tickets for major sights that require reservations.
- [ ] Save hotel addresses in Chinese characters for taxi / Didi drivers.
- [ ] Pack a Type-A power plug adapter and a power bank that meets airline rules.

## On arrival

- [ ] Keep passport accessible for hotel check-in and rail security.
- [ ] Test mobile payments on a small purchase before you rely on them.
- [ ] Prefer metro + HSR for city and intercity legs when time allows.
- [ ] Screenshot booking QR codes / confirmation numbers for offline use.

## Classic first route (reference only)

Many first trips use **Beijing → Xi'an → Shanghai**. Treat rail transfer days as full days. Open city guides on [relaxinchina.com](https://relaxinchina.com) for current booking notes.
